## Why This Matters

The single biggest predictor of whether an open-science practice survives a calendar year is whether it becomes embedded in the lab's normal workflow. A practice that is "the new way we do things" sticks. A practice that requires special effort each time eventually dies in the busy season.

This chapter will synthesize what the SLC learned across the year and offer concrete strategies for sustaining open-science practice past the end of the SLC.

## Topics to Cover

- Synthesizing practices into a coherent personal workflow
- Onboarding lab members to open-science practices
- Building incentives for trainees
- Cross-disciplinary collaboration patterns that worked for us
- Connecting to the broader open-science community at DePaul and beyond
- Sustaining momentum: planned check-ins, follow-on SLCs, ORS Lunch & Learn
