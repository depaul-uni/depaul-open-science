## Why This Matters

Open materials, the protocols, stimuli, code, instruments, and biological reagents that produced your results, are the fastest path to making research reusable. A paper alone is rarely enough to replicate. A paper plus the raw materials makes replication straightforward.

This chapter will be written and contributed by the SLC after the May 2026 meeting on open materials and collaborative protocols.

## Topics to Cover

- Strategies for sharing research materials, protocols, and code
- Platforms for materials sharing: protocols.io, OSF, Addgene, Materials Project
- Collaborative development of research protocols
- Discipline-specific norms (chemistry, psychology, linguistics, criminology)
- IRB and IP considerations when sharing materials
- Reagent and biological-material sharing through MTAs
