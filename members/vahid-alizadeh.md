## Research focus

I work in software engineering and applied AI/MLOps, with particular interest in reproducibility, automated software analysis, and the practical adoption of modern AI workflows by non-traditional users. My teaching includes graduate MLOps, OOP, Software Architecture and Globally Distributed Software Development.

## Why I joined this SLC

I have spent the past few years watching open-science practices become non-negotiable in computing-adjacent fields and uneven everywhere else. I wanted to bring DePaul faculty across disciplines into the same room to compare notes, learn from each other's constraints, and build something concrete enough to share with the broader community.

## Open-science practice I've found most relevant

Reproducible-analysis workflows (Chapter 4) and version control (Chapter 7). The discipline of one canonical version with every change attributable maps cleanly onto every form of scholarly output I produce, from grant applications to teaching materials to research code.
