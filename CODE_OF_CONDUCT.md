# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, members of the DePaul Open Science Scholarship Learning Community and contributors to this Implementation Guide, pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free, supportive, and intellectually honest experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to behaviors that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

**Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:**

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, methodologies, and disciplinary traditions
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the broader open-science movement
- Citing prior work generously

**Examples of unacceptable behavior:**

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Misrepresenting AI-generated content as human-authored without disclosure
- Plagiarism or fabrication of citations
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional academic setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the SLC facilitator at valizade@depaul.edu, or to DePaul University's Office of Equal Opportunity. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
