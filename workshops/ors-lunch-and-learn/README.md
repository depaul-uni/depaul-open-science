# ORS Lunch & Learn Workshop

The DePaul Office of Research Services hosts a Lunch & Learn series for faculty professional development. The Open Science SLC's year-end deliverable includes a workshop in this series, distilling the SLC's year of work into a single 60-90 minute session for the broader DePaul community.
