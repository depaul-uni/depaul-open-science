# v0.3.1 - Author List Update

**Release date:** June 11, 2026

## Summary

This is a metadata-only patch release. No content changes from v0.3.0.

## What changed

- **Author list:** Kayla Freemon removed from `CITATION.cff` and from the Zenodo metadata at her own request. Kayla remains a member of the 2025-26 SLC cohort and is acknowledged as such in the repo and members list; she has opted out of being listed as a formal co-author of the Implementation Guide.
- **Version metadata:** `CITATION.cff` bumped to v0.3.1 with release date 2026-06-11.
- **README:** version footer updated to reflect v0.3.1.

## Why a new release for this

Removing a co-author from a citation is a substantive metadata change that should be reflected in a citable deposit. Anyone who has already cited v0.3.0 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20633888) will see the original author list there; new citations should use v0.3.1's DOI going forward, and the concept DOI continues to resolve to the latest version.

## Content unchanged

All chapters, resource files, and supporting material from v0.3.0 are identical in v0.3.1. The next substantive release will be v1.0 after Meeting 9 (June 2026), which will include Chapter 9 and the year-end synthesis.
